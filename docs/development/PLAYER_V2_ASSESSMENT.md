# Player visual system V2: technical assessment and sequence

Date: 2026-11-24. Decision D-075 (foundation approved by the founder: MakeHuman CC0 base through the MPFB add-on). Replaces M1 as the visual target; M1 stays as the working fallback until V2 passes its gates.

## 1. Audit of what exists (M1 and earlier)
| Area | Today | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry | Everything is generated by our scripts from lofted rings, ellipsoids and tubes (`tools/blender/basketball/`). Library assets: none | No real anatomy: no muscle masses, joints, hands or facial structure; it reads as a mannequin (founder review of the M1 comparisons) |
| Faces | 18 heads = 6 parameter sets x 3 jaws, plus nose, lips, brow and eye overlays | Overlays sit on top of an egg; faces read as copies |
| Hands | Palm loft + 5 tubes, 2 bones per digit | Sausage fingers, no knuckles or nails |
| Clothing | Jersey and shorts are lofted shells offset from the torso profile | Reads as paint on a mannequin; no folds, seams, garment volume |
| Shoes | Lofted lozenge with tubes for laces | Not a basketball shoe silhouette |
| Hair | Caps and tubes | Helmet-like |
| Polygons | about 11,800 triangles body and clothes + 1,500 head and hair | Fine for budget, wasted on smooth tubes |
| Materials | Flat StandardMaterial3D colours; a weave shader on cloth; vertex-coloured eyes | No skin detail, no normal maps |
| Rig | 16 standard bones + 20 `x_` finger bones; weights painted by script | Weights are crude at shoulders and hips |
| Godot | `FcPlayerAssetLibrary` picks parts from `FcPlayerVisual.resolve()`; `FcHandPoses`; `FcJerseyGraphics`; `FcPlayerRender`; 34 states via `FcBroadcastPose` + `FcRigRetarget` | All reusable for V2 |
| Performance | 31 characters + crowd: 5.3 ms avg | Ten players are easy; room for denser meshes |

What M1 got right and V2 keeps: the identity resolver, the asset library and cache, the retarget layer (any rest, any rotation), hand poses, texture-driven numbers, portraits from the 3D model, the validator gate, CI rebuilds, the promotion flag.

## 2. The new foundation
- **MakeHuman base mesh** (CC0) generated headlessly by **MPFB 2.0.18** (GPL, a build tool only: nothing of it ships) inside the portable Blender 4.5. `tools/blender/install_mpfb.ps1` installs both with pinned SHA-256 checksums into the user profile, never into the repository.
- **Only CC0 assets** (the MPFB core data and the `makehuman_system_assets` pack, whose files state "explicitly released as CC0"). CC-BY packs are not used. Everything is logged in `assets/ART_PROVENANCE.md`.
- **Rig**: MPFB's `game_engine` rig (53 bones, 3 per finger, toes), professionally weighted, converted to our standard: the 16 standard bone names, our rest pose (arms hanging), the extra bones kept as `x_` extensions (spine, clavicles, three finger joints, toes).
- **Variation without copies**: ONE base character per level of detail carries named morph targets (body: muscle, weight, shoulders, chest, arms, legs; face: head shape, jaw, chin, cheeks, forehead, nose, eyes, brows, ears, lips, asymmetry). A player's morph weights are computed deterministically from `FcPlayerVisual.resolve()` and the body archetype; Godot bakes the mix ONCE per player (`bake_mesh_from_current_blend_shape_mix`), so the per-frame cost equals a plain skinned mesh. Height is the model scale; wingspan and leg ratio are bone-length scales; mass and build are morphs (not a uniform scale).
- **Garments and equipment** are separate meshes fitted to the body in Blender and given the same body morphs, so they follow every build.
- **Levels of detail**: high (full base mesh), medium (MakeHuman proxy), low (low proxy), switched by camera distance.

## 3. Constraints found
- Godot gl_compatibility: no subsurface scattering; skin shading is a custom shader (wrap lighting, detail texture tinted by the identity skin tone).
- Morph targets in GLB are large: sparse export, normals kept, only meaningful shapes.
- The rig standard's parents (`l_shoulder` under `torso`) need one explicit extension: a standard bone may sit under a chain of `x_` bones that descends from its standard parent (model standard 2). The validator gains that rule; version-1 models are checked exactly as before.

## 4. Sequence (each milestone: build, tests, renders front/side/back/face, in-game screenshots, fixes, docs, commit, push)
M2 anatomy and body types -> M7 rig conversion and deformation (needed by everything after) -> M3 faces -> M8 skin and eye materials -> M4 hair and facial hair -> M5 garments -> M6 equipment -> M9 Godot integration, LOD, all modes -> final comparison against M1.
