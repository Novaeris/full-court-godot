# Basketball authenticity and data-integrity audit (Stage 2)

Date: 2026-11-22. Baseline: commit `7d05455` (Stage 1 stabilization). **Read-only:** nothing under `src/` or `data/` was changed. The one addition is the evidence tool `tools/audit_identity.ps1` (`.gd`), which only measures. Companion documents: [BASKETBALL_DATA_INTEGRITY_MATRIX.md](BASKETBALL_DATA_INTEGRITY_MATRIX.md) (who owns which field) and [../plans/BASKETBALL_AUTHENTICITY_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](../plans/BASKETBALL_AUTHENTICITY_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) (what to do about it). Older audits: [../INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md](../INTEGRATION_AUDIT.md), [../STABILIZATION_AUDIT.md](../STABILIZATION_AUDIT.md).

Each finding has one **label** (Confirmed defect, Architectural risk, Recommendation, Unverified, Decision), one **severity** (P0 to P3 as in the stabilization audit) and one **layer** (Structural, Basketball, Historical). Evidence is a file and line, a test, or a measurement; "measured" means `tools/audit_identity.ps1` over 20 leagues generated from seeds 5000 to 5019 (600 teams, 10,200 players).

## 1. How identity is managed today (summary)
- A league player is one `FcPlayer` (`src/data/player/player.gd`): id, status, positions, `identity` (name, birth date, country), `physical` (height, weight, wingspan, handedness), attributes. Team membership is `FcTeam.roster.player_ids` (`src/data/team/roster.gd:11`); the contract is separate (`FcContract`, `world.active_contract_of`).
- **There is no jersey number on a league player.** Numbers exist in three unrelated places: the Career profile's chosen `jersey_number` (`career_profile.gd:24`), a hashed display number `FcFranchiseHistory.jersey_for(id)` (`franchise_history.gd:244`) used only to label retired numbers, and `0` passed by the profile view model for Owner players and Dynasty cards (`player_presentation.gd:72,157`).
- A team's look is `FcTeamBranding` (three colours and a logo key) plus `FcTeamIdentity` (nickname, abbreviation). Uniforms are not data: they are derived in the UI from the branding colours (`uniform_preview.gd`, `player_figure*.gd`, `team_look.gd`).
- Integrity is checked by per-class `validate()` and `FcWorld.validate(season)` (`world.gd:264`), which **already** checks: one roster per player, no retired player on a roster, a roster player has an active contract with that team, roster size at most the league maximum, one active contract per player, valid cities, arenas, leagues and seasons. It runs when a save is written or loaded (`save_manager.gd:81,208`) and in the app (`app.gd:117`). My earlier plan listed some of these as gaps; they are not.

## 2. Findings
| ID | P | Layer | Label | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2-JER-1 | P1 | Structural / Historical | Confirmed defect | League players have no stored number, and no roster path (sign, release, trade, draft, offseason) knows numbers exist. A search of `src/` finds `jersey` only in the Career files, `franchise_history.gd`, `storylines.gd`, `legacy.gd`, `owner_history.gd` and the player renderers; none of `game_controller.gd`, `trade_desk.gd`, `trades.gd`, `offseason.gd`, `expansion.gd` mention it. Any surface that shows "the number of a roster player" has nothing consistent to show |
| S2-JER-2 | P1 | Structural | Confirmed defect | The hashed display number collides inside a team. Measured: **80 of 600 teams (13.3%) have at least one pair of teammates with the same hashed number**, 300 colliding pairs in total, worst team 5 pairs. Only retired numbers are made unique (`franchise_history.gd:232-238`). Showing those numbers as player numbers would put two teammates in the same number |
| S2-JER-3 | P2 | Historical | Confirmed defect | A Career player's chosen number is never checked against the numbers the franchise has retired: the only consumers of `retired_numbers_all` are `legacy.gd`, `storylines.gd` and `owner_history.gd`; `career_controller.gd` (join, line 165) does not call it. A created player can wear a number a franchise retired |
| S2-JER-4 | P2 | Structural | Unverified | Number 0 is overloaded: the Career default is 0 (`player_build.gd`) and the creator spinner allows 0 to 99 (`career_creator.gd:137`), so "no number" and "number zero" cannot be told apart. Whether the renderers draw "0" or nothing for `jersey = 0` was not opened. Whether "00" exists anywhere: no (range is 0 to 99) |
| S2-JER-5 | P3 | Structural | Architectural risk | The bump `while used.has(number): number = number % 99 + 1` (`franchise_history.gd:234-237`) cannot end if all 99 numbers are retired for one franchise. Unreachable in normal play (it needs 99 retired numbers) |
| S2-HIS-1 | P2 | Historical | Confirmed (impact Unverified) | One stat line and one team per player per season, the team of his **latest** game (`season_stats.gd:10,24`), copied to `world.careers` with replace-by-year (`world.gd:163-176`). A player who moves mid-season has the whole season credited to his last team, which feeds `FcFranchiseHistory.primary_team` / `with_team` (tenure, retired-number eligibility, franchise leaders). AI trades run only in the offseason (`offseason.gd:95`, `game_controller.gd:934-944`), so the moves that can split a season are the user's own trades, signings and releases. How often that happens in real play was **not measured** |
| S2-TEAM-1 | P2 | Structural | Confirmed defect | Team colours can be unreadable against each other. Measured: **73 of 600 teams (12.2%)** have a primary and secondary colour with WCAG contrast below 3:1 (none below 1.5:1). Any jersey number drawn in the secondary colour on the primary would be hard to read on those teams. No check exists in `FcTeamBranding.validate` (it only checks the hex format) |
| S2-TEAM-2 | P3 | Structural | Recommendation | No test or validator requires unique team abbreviations or arena names within a league. Measured: none duplicated in 600 teams (the generator avoids it), so this is a guard, not a defect |
| S2-ROS-1 | P3 | Structural | Architectural risk | `FcTradeDesk._arrive` ignores the result of `roster.add` and assumes `active_contract_of(pid)` is not null (`trade_desk.gd:137-140`). It is safe today because `propose` checks ownership, no-trade consent, contracts (null contract returns "cannot be traded"), roster maximum and minimum and the cap before executing (`trade_desk.gd:31-94`), and removes before adding. Duplicate ids inside `give_ids` are not rejected; the UI never sends them |
| S2-ROS-2 | P2 | Structural | Unverified | `FcWorld.validate` checks the roster maximum but not the minimum or position coverage. Measured at generation: every team has exactly 15 players (rules: 13 to 15), and **no team lacks a primary at any of the five positions**. After a played season this was not measured |
| S2-PLY-1 | P3 | Basketball | Confirmed (informational) | Physical plausibility, measured over 10,200 players: heights by position look right (PG 170-202 cm, median 187; SG 177-212; SF 185-221; PF 192-221; C 199-227, median 213), weights 60-146 kg, no BMI below 18.5 or above 32, ages 19 to 39 (median 27), left-handed 9.7%, no centre under 195 cm, no point guard over 205 cm, no secondary position two or more steps from the primary, no secondary equal to the primary, no invalid birth date, no empty country, **no duplicate full name inside a league**. One oddity: **171 players (1.7%) have a wingspan more than 2 cm shorter than their height**; real players do have negative reach but rarely; informational |
| S2-PLY-2 | P3 | Basketball | Confirmed (gap) | There is no draft year or draft position on `FcPlayer`; "draft class" can only come from league history, not from the player |
| S2-RUL-1 | P2 | Basketball | Unverified | `FcCourtLayout.attacks_right` gives overtime periods (5 and up) the first-half basket direction (`court_layout.gd:26-30`). Whether that matches the chosen fictional-league convention was not decided; it is presentation only |
| S2-RUL-2 | P3 | Basketball | Confirmed (scope) | The simulation has no timeouts, shot clock, jump ball or technical fouls (`game_simulator.gd` header, search of `src/sim`). This is scope, not a defect; any broadcast graphic must not invent them |
| S2-XMODE-1 | P2 | Structural | Architectural risk | Identity is intentionally separate per mode: Owner and Career use the shared `FcPlayer`; Dynasty cards are static content rows with no franchise (`subject_for_card`). Nothing is shared with Dynasty except the simulation and the visual resolver; a card's number is `0` today. A number for cards would be a content-format change |
| S2-XMODE-2 | P2 | Structural | Unverified | Creator Studio content passes `FcContentValidator`, which validates release structure; whether it applies the same identity rules (name length, position, height range) as generation was not compared line by line |

## 3. Areas examined and their result
- **A. Jersey numbers:** see S2-JER-1 to 5. Valid range (Career 0 to 99, hashed 1 to 99); duplicates inside a team (measured); assignment at generation (none); availability on signing or trade (not consulted); retired numbers (franchise-specific by construction, keyed by team in `retired_numbers_all`, not globally blocked; that is correct); history of numbers (none); changes during a career (none possible); cross-mode consistency (none); rendering (only the Career hub title and the retired-number cards print a number; the Owner roster table has no number column).
- **B. Player information:** S2-PLY-1, S2-PLY-2. Name convention: first and last name plus optional nickname (`FcPersonIdentity`); no duplicates inside a league. Age is computed from the birth date (`player.gd:46`).
- **C. Team and franchise identity:** abbreviations 2 to 4 uppercase letters and unique in the measured leagues; franchise ids are stable integers from `FcIdGenerator`; colours S2-TEAM-1; arenas unique in the measured leagues; conference and division assignments checked by `FcLeague.validate` and `FcWorld.validate` (0 problems over 20 leagues); history is derived from `world.careers` (S2-HIS-1).
- **D. Roster and transaction integrity:** the existing validator catches a player on two rosters, a retired player on a roster and a roster player without an active contract with the team; trades are pre-validated and execute remove-then-add (S2-ROS-1). Not exercised here: signing and release in `game_controller.gd` (about lines 1335-1395) and the offseason trims were **not read line by line**; they are covered by `test_free_agent_signing`, `test_trades`, `test_trade_desk` and by `world.validate` after a played season in `tools/play_seasons.gd` and `owner_stress`.
- **E. Uniforms:** no home, away or alternate data model; colours come from branding; number placement is a renderer matter. Contrast problem S2-TEAM-1. Accessories, shoes, socks and compression gear are not modelled (a gap and a design option; D-068 plans cosmetic sleeves for online Career only).
- **F. Rules and statistics:** `test_game_events.gd` proves the event log reproduces the box score by period, the final and every player line, that lineups stay legal all game and that the clock only runs down inside a period. Season statistics are summed from boxes (`season_stats.gd`). Not re-derived independently here.
- **G. Cross-mode:** S2-XMODE-1 and 2.
- **H. Broadcast and presentation:** the scorebug and replay read the box score and event rows (one source); there is no player-number overlay today. Nothing printed names a player by a different id than the rows.

## 4. What is correct and should be left alone
The one-roster-per-player and contract-agreement validation, the retired-number rules per franchise, trade pre-validation, generation-level plausibility of names and physiques, and the separation of person (`FcPlayer`) from team membership (roster) from contract.

## 5. Not verified
Renderer behaviour for number 0 and for league players; a played season's roster state against `world.validate` beyond what `play_seasons` and `owner_stress` check; the frequency of mid-season moves; Studio content against generation rules; draft-year questions (no field).
