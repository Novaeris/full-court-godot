Privacy

Draft, not legal advice. This page describes what the game does today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must be reviewed and completed before online accounts are switched on (see docs/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md).

Today

The current game is played offline. Your saved games and settings stay on your computer. The game does not send data anywhere.

When online play arrives

Online features will use an account (an e-mail address and a display name) so you can play with others. We plan to keep this to what is needed to run the game, never sell it, and let you delete your account. The final policy will say exactly what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion.
