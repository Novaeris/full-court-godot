Run the franchise. Build the player. Collect the dynasty.

Full Court is a basketball management simulation for PC with original teams and players, real cities, and games you can watch play out.

Download for Windows

Free to play. Early build: Windows may show an "unknown publisher" warning (choose More info, then Run anyway).

Three ways to play

Owner Mode

Control every part of a franchise for up to 50 years: finances, staff, trades, drafts and the court. You can't be fired, but you can change teams.

Career Mode

Create one player and take them through a career of about 20 years: training, decisions, public image and a legacy.

Dynasty Mode

Collect cards, open packs, set your lineup, climb a ranked ladder and enter tournaments.

Download and install

  1. Download the zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it anywhere and double-click FullCourt.exe. Nothing needs to be installed.
  3. Your saved games stay on your computer.

Windows only for now. If no release is listed yet, the first public build has not been published.
